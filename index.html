Scopus & Web of Science conference listings

A short, general overview of what these indexes mean and how conference publications typically move toward them.

Scopus

Scopus is a large abstract and citation database of peer-reviewed literature maintained by Elsevier. Conference proceedings are sometimes submitted by organizers for evaluation and inclusion, but inclusion is decided independently by Scopus, not by any listing service.

Web of Science

Web of Science, maintained by Clarivate, is another major citation index covering journals and select conference proceedings. Coverage decisions are made by Clarivate's editorial process and can change over time.

Publication routes

Accepted papers may be published through conference proceedings, invited journal publication, or special issues, depending on the individual event's arrangements with its publisher.

Indexing verification: Indexing and publication information may be provided by event organizers or available sources. Researchers should independently verify current Scopus/Web of Science coverage and publication arrangements before submitting papers or making payments. Always confirm directly through the relevant indexing database before submitting or paying conference fees.
